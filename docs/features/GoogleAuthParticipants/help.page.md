# Google Auth for Participants — Guide

Guide page content for the join flow. Each section explains the UI element and recommended actions.

## Sign in with Google button

Use this option if you're a team member with a Google account. Signing in with Google means you'll be recognized across devices and future sprints — if you switch laptops or close your browser, you can pick up right where you left off. Your display name is pulled from your Google profile (first name). You don't need to choose a name.

## Join as Guest section

Use this option if you don't have a Google account or if you're an external participant joining for a single session. You'll pick a display name and your session will be stored in your browser. Your session is tied to this specific browser — if you clear your browser data or switch devices, you'll need to rejoin with a new name. Your cards and votes from the original session won't carry over.

## Guest name input

Enter the name you want other participants to see during the retro. Names must be unique within the sprint — if someone else already took your preferred name, you'll see an error and need to pick a different one. The list of participants who have already joined is shown above the input so you can see which names are taken.

## Auto-join notification

When you open a sprint link while signed in with Google, you're automatically joined using your Google first name. No action is needed — you'll go straight into the retro room. If your name happened to match someone who already joined, a number was added to make it unique (e.g., "Ana 2").
