# Google Auth for Participants — Tooltips

Tooltip text for UI elements. Each tooltip is under 150 characters, shown on info icon hover.

## Sign in with Google button

Sign in with your Google account to join with your real name. Your identity persists across devices and sprints.

## Join as Guest section

Join without a Google account. Pick a display name — your session is tied to this browser only.

## Guest name input

Choose a display name. Names must be unique within this sprint.

## Auto-join notification

You were automatically joined using your Google profile name.
